## Plain summary (not the legal bit)

Courier is software for schools. You install it on your own server and use it to send short notices to the
Windows computers in your school. It is free, for any number of computers. **It is not an emergency system.**
A notice can arrive late, or not at all, and it can go unseen: a computer may be off, asleep, locked or
signed out, or the network may be down. Never use Courier as the only way to warn people about a fire, a
lockdown, an evacuation or anything else where a delay could put someone at risk. The notices you write, who
